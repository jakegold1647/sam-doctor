Questions

Does SAM Doctor upload my logs?

No. The free alpha processes the file locally. Review any report before sharing it outside your team.

Can it change IAM policies or deploy resources?

No. It provides evidence and read-only verification steps only. You remain responsible for every change.

Who is it for?

Solo developers, freelancers, and small teams that deploy Python or other applications through AWS and GitHub Actions.
